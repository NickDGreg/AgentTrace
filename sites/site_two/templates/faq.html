Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Use the Sign up button, complete the registration form, and then sign in.

Where do I find crypto deposit addresses?

After login, open Deposit, choose method and amount, then continue to Payment.

Can I access dashboard pages without signing in?

No. All account and dashboard pages are behind the authentication wall.

Which payment methods are available?

Bitcoin Cash, Ethereum, and Bitcoin checkout flows are available.
